# 🧬 longevity-coach

> A Claude skill that merges Longevity Science, Neuroscience, and Life Coaching into one integrated advisor — mechanism-first, anti-fluff, with a single concrete action at the end of every response.

---

## The Problem With Most Health Advice

Generic: *"Exercise more. Sleep better. Reduce stress."*

Useless — because it skips the two things that actually change behavior:
1. **WHY** your biology works the way it does
2. **What specific barrier** is stopping you from acting on what you already know

`longevity-coach` fixes this.

---

## What This Skill Does

Every response follows one model:

```
WHY the body/brain works this way      →  Neuroscience
× HOW LONG it affects your lifespan   →  Longevity Science
× HOW TO ACTUALLY CHANGE IT           →  Coaching
= Sustainable, science-backed transformation
```

It connects at least two domains in every answer. Biology without behavior change is a lecture. Coaching without mechanism is motivation porn. This skill does neither.

---

## Response Structure (Every Time)

1. **Identify the intersection** — biology question or behavior question?
2. **Explain the mechanism** — the biological WHY before any recommendation
3. **Give a specific protocol** — time-bound, measurable, not vague
4. **Address the barrier** — what neurological/psychological reason is stopping you?
5. **One action today** — non-negotiable. Not a menu of options. One.

## What Makes This Different

**Mechanism before recommendation — always.**

❌ Generic advice:
> "Exercise is good for your brain."

✅ This skill:
> "Zone 2 cardio 3×/week elevates BDNF ~30%, directly promoting hippocampal neurogenesis — the brain's primary mechanism for growing new memory neurons. Here's the 8-week protocol."

---

## Hard Rules Inside the Skill

- No pharmaceutical recommendations without explicit medical supervision caveat
- No neuroscience buzzwords without explaining the actual mechanism
- No longevity advice without acknowledging individual biomarker variation
- No coaching before assessing nervous system state (window of tolerance)
- No confusing correlation with causation in longevity research
- **No "biohacking" optimization culture** — chronic optimization anxiety is itself anti-longevity
